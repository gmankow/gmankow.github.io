Senior Design / AI & hardware design

Agentic AI for Circuit Card Design

Early circuit card design requires exploring design tradeoffs, researching previous designs, identifying components and circuit board materials, and defining requirements. Our project explores how an agentic AI system can automate and speed up these tasks to support hardware designers. Another goal is to enable AI agents to update a KiCad design using the information provided, so the resulting design reflects that information as accurately as possible.

My role
LLM Model Research Lead and Model Context Protocol (MCP) Subject Matter Expert.
Skills and knowledge gained
I learned about MCP servers, vision models for reading schematics, KiCad netlists, and local AI development.
Big picture contribution
My work focuses on vision models that read schematics and an MCP server that connects agents to the project's PathRAG knowledge system and KiCad MCP server. These connections support the goal of helping agents use the provided information to make accurate updates to KiCad designs, while helping hardware designers gather information and explore circuit card designs more efficiently.
