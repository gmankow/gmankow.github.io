Senior design

Agentic AI for Circuit Card Design

Our team is building an AI agent system to help with early circuit card design. It will help designers research previous designs, compare components and board materials, and define requirements. We also want the agents to use that information to update designs in KiCad.

My role
LLM Model Research Lead and Model Context Protocol (MCP) Subject Matter Expert.
Skills and knowledge gained
I learned about MCP servers, vision models for reading schematics, KiCad netlists, and local AI development.
Big picture contribution
I work on vision models that read schematics and an MCP server that connects agents to our PathRAG knowledge system and KiCad MCP server. This gives the agents access to the project information and design tools they need. The goal is to reduce the time hardware designers spend gathering information and making early design changes.

Our team website has weekly reports. Technical documents will be added as the project progresses.
